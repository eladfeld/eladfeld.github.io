Research

My work focuses on the security and robustness of modern machine learning systems, with an emphasis on large language models and AI agents deployed in real-world environments.

LLM & agent securityPrompt injection, promptware, agentic botnets
Adversarial MLAttacks on perception and learning-based systems
Model-agnostic defensesLightweight detectors that work across models
Empirical benchmarksMeasuring robustness under realistic conditions
